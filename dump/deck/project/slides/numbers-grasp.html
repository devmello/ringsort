For "what does more training actually change?". "We measured where the ring was in the gripper camera at the moment the gripper closed, and compared it with where it was in our own demonstrations. At 10,000 steps purple was 75 pixels off and red 100; the gripper closed beside the ring. With more training it closes where we did: purple 18 pixels at 50,000, red 30 at 40,000, teal 10 at 20,000. Every placed pick closed within 20 pixels. The model is learning how the gripper has to move to land on the ring."
