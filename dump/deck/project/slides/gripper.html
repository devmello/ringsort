The gripper knows when it's holding something

A demonstration: held a ring

Shaded: the gap while holding (about 13 units, 1.9 s)

A model run: missed

No gap anywhere: it closed on nothing

Solid: where the gripper got to. Dashed: where it was told to go. Up = more open.

Deep dive, 20 s, or keep for questions. "This is the main idea. LeRobot records two numbers for the gripper on every frame: where it was told to go, and where it got to. When it closes on a ring, it can't get there: that shaded gap. When it closes on nothing, the two agree. A gap that vanishes while it's still told to hold means the part slipped. Where the base points when it opens says whether that was over the right bin."
