Sorting comes before packing

Mixed parts are sorted into one bin per part type. Packing pulls kits from those bins.

A part in the wrong bin, or one left behind, becomes a wrong kit downstream, where it costs far more to find.

Robots that learn by demonstration fail silently. The model does not know it missed.
