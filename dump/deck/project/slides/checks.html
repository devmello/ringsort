Every pick is checked by something the model doesn't control

The gripper's own record

Holding a part stops the gripper closing all the way. No gap means it picked up nothing. No extra sensor.

The stand camera

Which parts are on the table, whether each sits where it was taught, what is in a bin, whether the camera moved.

The operator, only when needed

A miss or a drop stops the cell and calls the operator: retry, placed by hand, skip or cancel.
