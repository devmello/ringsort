2:30–2:45 (Technical execution). "The biggest thing we learned: good data matters more than anything else. When we recorded teal, everything stayed consistent: the camera didn't move by more than 3 pixels across all 45 takes, and the ring started in nearly the same spot every time. Teal's model worked at 20,000 training steps. Purple's camera moved three times while we recorded it, and its model needed 50,000. Red's camera moved after we recorded it, and it needed 40,000." If asked: teal was also recorded last, when we were most practiced, so it's the consistency of the whole session, not one variable.
